# Bird Control BC logo directions

These are vector concept boards for selection. They do not replace the live site logo until a direction is approved.

## A. Refined Coastline Wing

Evolves the current finalist with a sharper bird profile, a more architectural protected opening, and a restrained exclusion grid. It retains the strongest balance of local character, service relevance, premium tone, and horizontal wordmark performance.

## B. BC Monogram + Bird Exclusion

Prioritizes compact recognition by combining the initials with a bird interruption and exclusion lines. It is strongest for social avatars, uniforms, and vehicle identification, but the service story is less immediate than A or C.

## C. Architectural Bird Mark

Combines a bird silhouette with a balcony edge, upright, and taut net lines. It communicates property protection and contractor capability most directly, though it has less inherent BC/coastal identity than A or B.

Recommendation: keep A as the lead direction. It performs consistently across every required application without relying on a literal monogram or a generic contractor symbol. C is the strongest alternate if the brand should emphasize architecture and exclusion over regional identity.
