# Bird Control BC Option A Refinements

These concept systems are isolated from the live website brand assets. Each direction includes:

- `primary-horizontal.svg`
- `stacked.svg`
- `social-avatar.svg`
- `favicon.svg`
- `monochrome.svg`
- `dark-background.svg`
- `vehicle-uniform.svg`
- `comparison-board.svg`

## A1: Premium Coastal

The strongest overall continuation of Option A. It keeps the most flowing Coastline Wing profile, uses one quiet ledge line, and gives the icon more weight beside the wordmark.

## A2: Premium Architectural

The most contractor-oriented direction. The bird is slightly more controlled and the single ledge corner communicates property protection without adding net grids or technical linework.

## A3: Bold Social-First

The strongest small-format direction. Its more compact silhouette and heavier ledge remain recognizable in Instagram, Facebook, Google Business, and favicon crops.

## Recommended Hybrid

Use A1 for the primary horizontal, stacked, dark-background, monochrome, and vehicle applications. Use the bolder A3 social-avatar and favicon treatment for small digital placements. This preserves the premium coastal identity while improving recognition where the full wordmark cannot appear.

No refinement in this folder is connected to the live header or metadata until a direction is approved.
