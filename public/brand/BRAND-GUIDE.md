# Bird Control BC — Brand Kit

## Core identity
Premium, local, practical bird-control contractor for Vancouver + the Lower Mainland. The mark must read immediately as a **bird in flight**, never as a fish/dolphin.

## Palette
- Forest Green `#0F3B2E` — primary
- Olive `#7A8F4A` — accent
- Warm Ivory `#EDE6DD` — light background
- Charcoal `#222222` — neutral text
- Slate `#6B6B6B` — secondary text

## Typography
- Display/headings: Playfair Display Semibold (Georgia fallback)
- UI/body: Inter Regular/Medium (Arial fallback)

## Logo system
- `final/logo-horizontal.svg` — website header, proposals, invoices
- `final/logo-stacked.svg` — square/vertical placements
- `final/icon.svg` — social avatar, favicon, app icon
- `final/logo-monochrome.svg` — stamps, embroidery, one-colour production
- `final/logo-dark-background.svg` — dark signage/backgrounds
- `final/logo-vehicle-uniform.svg` — vans, uniforms, field collateral

## Usage rules
1. Keep clear space around the mark equal to roughly the bird head height.
2. Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, gradients, outlines, or extra colours.
3. Use the icon without wording below ~80px wide.
4. Use the horizontal logo for normal website/print placements.
5. Prefer Forest Green on Warm Ivory; reverse to Ivory on Forest Green.
6. Keep the bird silhouette recognizable: raised feathered wing, distinct head/eye, short beak and tail.

## Marketing look
Photography should show real balconies, netting, spikes, cleanup, technicians, strata/commercial buildings and recognizable Lower Mainland context. Keep layouts clean, premium and evidence-led rather than cartoonish pest-control graphics.

## Messaging hierarchy
Primary: `Bird Control BC`
Location: `Vancouver · Lower Mainland`
Service line: `Bird Control · Cleanup · Exclusion`
CTA: `Send Photos for a Free Quote`
